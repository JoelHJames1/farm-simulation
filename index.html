Frequently asked questions

Is my farm saved?
Yes. Your progress is saved automatically on your device. Deleting the app deletes your saved farms.

I bought gems or coins but didn't receive them.
Open the game, tap the pause button, then Settings, then Restore purchases. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I unlock new farms?
Tap PLAY on the main menu to open the farm map. Each farm unlocks at a farm level, or earlier with gems.

My workers stopped working.
Make sure your farm has a barn (to store harvests) and that you can pay wages. Tap a worker, then tap a field, pen or building to give them a job.
