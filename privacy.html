Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Summary

Farm Simulation does not ask for, collect or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (farms, coins, gems, level and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. Deleting the app removes this data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment card or billing details. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Technical data collected by the game engine

The game is built with Unity. The Unity engine may send anonymous technical information, such as device model, operating system version and performance statistics, to Unity Technologies to help them improve the engine. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used by us for advertising or tracking. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Email joel.hernandez.james@gmail.com.
